## LinkedIn
Screen fatigue and dry eyes are all too common when you spend your workday at a computer.

I built EyeRest 🌿 — a quiet, lightweight Windows companion designed around the 20-20-20 rule: every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds.

Unlike traditional reminder apps that aggressively hijack your screen while you're in the middle of typing, EyeRest gives you a gentle 10-second heads up, then dims smoothly into a 20-second breathing pause with a soothing singing bowl chime. It never steals keyboard focus, lives quietly in your system tray, and is completely free and open source.

Check it out on GitHub: https://github.com/HassaanGhayas/eye-rest
