# Hyperframes Composition Brief: EyeRest 🌿

## Objective
Create a fancy, high-end launch brag video for EyeRest that speaks normally to everyday computer users, focusing on eye wellness, calm productivity, and non-intrusive design.

## Output
- Composition directory: `brag-output/composition/`
- Rendered video: `brag-output/brag.mp4`
- Format: landscape — 1920x1080, 30fps
- Duration: 19.50 seconds

## Source Material
- Product: EyeRest 🌿
- Theme: 20-20-20 rule (every 20 minutes, look 20 feet away for 20 seconds)
- Key UI features:
  - 10-second gentle floating toast notification
  - Dimmed calm meditation overlay with breathing circle and 20s countdown
  - Tibetan singing bowl 432 Hz chime
  - Focus safety: never interrupts typing, dismissible with Esc
- Copy requirements:
  - Normal, human language (no tech jargon, no memory statistics, no Electron mentions)
  - Clear, relatable tone

## Creative Direction
- Tone: Polished, fancy, zen, human
- Visual Look:
  - Deep midnight background `#0B0F15` with soft radiant gradients
  - Glassmorphic card surfaces with fine luminous emerald/mint borders `#5EEAD4`
  - High-end typography with elegant tracking and optical hierarchy
  - Subtle breathing animations and ambient lighting
- Avoid:
  - Any mention of Electron, Stretchly, or competing tools
  - Jargon like RAM, memory, Win32 APIs, Rust, compiler, or telemetry
  - Cramped spacing or harsh contrasting blocks
