# Brag Plan: EyeRest 🌿

## What is this app?
EyeRest is a peaceful, unobtrusive Windows desktop companion that helps anyone staring at screens all day protect their vision with gentle 20-20-20 eye breaks.

## The angle
Focus on everyday human well-being, calm productivity, and zero disruption. Staring at screens causes real eye strain; EyeRest gives you a gentle 10-second heads-up, followed by a soothing 20-second breathing pause to rest your eyes—without ever stealing your typing focus or breaking your flow. Absolutely zero technical jargon, no competitor bashing, just pure zen utility.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
"Your eyes work hard all day." A relatable, human truth that resonates instantly with anyone who spends hours at a computer. Accompanied by a gentle ambient pulse and warm typography.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The gentle 10-second nudge**: A quiet corner toast that lets you wrap up your sentence or thoughts before the break begins.
- **The calm breathing circle**: The screen softly dims into a relaxing meditation space with an expanding breathing circle, 20-second countdown, and a 432 Hz Tibetan singing bowl chime.
- **Zero disruption**: Never steals your cursor or keyboard focus—press Esc anytime or let yourself take a breath.

## Outro / punchline
"Give your eyes the rest they deserve."
EyeRest · A quiet companion for Windows · 100% Free & Open Source

## User flow worth showing
Working peacefully ➔ 10-second corner notification floats in ➔ Screen gently dims into the zen breathing overlay with chime ➔ Quick 20-second refresh and back to work seamlessly.
